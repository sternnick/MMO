# MMO Research — Finding the Right Game

> **Goal:** An MMO where **time invested = permanent progress** — level-ups and gear persist, with **no wipes, no seasonal resets, no prestige/level resets**. PC only. Genre-agnostic.
>
> **Player profile:** Comes from *Lineage 2*, *World of Warcraft*, *New World*, *Elder Scrolls Online*. Loved **ESO's model** — low time-sacrifice, forgiving of a small budget (~8 hrs/month), and enjoyable **without paying** (no pay-to-win wall).
>
> **Last updated:** 2026-08-31 · **Verified against:** official game sites, Wikipedia, UESP, PC Gamer, EVE News, OSRS homepage.

---

## The 3 Filters That Matter

1. **Persistent progress** — your character, level, and gear stick forever. No server/seasonal wipes, no "prestige" resets that lower your level, no forced gear resets.
2. **Small-time-friendly** — you can progress and enjoy the game with only a few hours a month.
3. **F2P-viable / no pay-to-win** — the game is fun without spending money, and spending money doesn't buy a competitive edge.

A game passes only if it satisfies **all three**.

# 1. Old School RuneScape (OSRS) — ⭐ Best Fit

**Links:** [Official site](https://oldschool.runescape.com/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)

### What it is
Jagex's retro-styled MMORPG, launched **22 Feb 2013** from an August 2007 backup, now a separately maintained live game shaped by **player polls**. Thriving in 2026: **94,035 players online** on the official site, record **240,000+ concurrent** (Aug 2025, via GameSpot).

### (1) Free-to-play vs. membership
- **Model:** "Limited free-to-play mode; full access requires membership." Membership can be bought **directly from Jagex** or through **Old School Bonds** (tradable in-game items redeemable for membership). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)
- **F2P unlocks:** limited area, a subset of skills/quests/combat. **Membership unlocks:** the full world, all quests, all skills, all raids, all PvP worlds, trading, banks.
- **Price:** historically **~£6.99 / $9.99 / €7.99 per month** with discounts for 3/6/12-month terms *(2026 price not tool-verified — confirm at checkout)*.

### (2) How to start
- **Clients:** desktop (Windows/macOS), **mobile** (Android/iOS, cross-play), and **Steam** (since 24 Feb 2021). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)
- **First steps:** create account → "Download Old School" → pick a world (new players often start on a non-PvP/novice world) → starting tutorial → begin training a skill (Woodcutting/Fishing/Mining) on the Attack + Strength + Hitpoints combat base. Use the official **"New Player Guide"** link.

### (3) Skill system — real-time progression
- XP-based leveling: skills improve over **wall-clock time and repetition** — your time counts even if you play slowly. [OSRS homepage](https://oldschool.runescape.com/)
- **Max level 99 per skill**; **Sailing** is the newest (24th) skill, launched **Nov 2025**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)

### (4) Content
- Full skilling ecosystem, quests, minigames, **Ironman modes** (standard / ultimate — no trading), **clan houses**, and **raids** (God Wars Dungeon, Fossil Island, etc.) available to all members. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)
- **PvP:** risk-it-all PvP worlds + the Wilderness; **Deadman Mode** = a separate server with accelerated rates and death penalties (loss of gear/levels) — *not* the persistent world.

### (5) Why it fits an 8-hr/month player
- **Player-directed progression** — "the challenging levelling system," no daily/weekly pressure, no FOMO. Content is added incrementally via **70%-threshold player polls** since 2022, so you can always catch up. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)
- **Cross-platform** (mobile + desktop + Steam) lets you use small windows of time.

### (6) 2026 news
- **26 Aug 2026** — "Mad Angel Combat Achievements"; more Sailing/Loot Sacks changes. [OSRS homepage](https://oldschool.runescape.com/)
- **Nov 2025** — Sailing (first new skill) launched. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)

> **Must know:** OSRS is **poll-driven and community-controlled** — in 2019 a proposed pay-to-win feature was rejected, and Jagex committed to **no microtransactions/pay-to-win**. Your progress on the **permanent world** is truly permanent.

# 2. Guild Wars 2 — ⭐ No Subscription

**Links:** [Official site](https://www.guildwars2.com/en/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guild_Wars_2) · [PC Gamer — GW3 coverage](https://www.pcgamer.com/guild-wars-3/)

### What it is
ArenaNet/NCSoft MMORPG, launched **28 Aug 2012**, set in Tyria. **Free-to-play, no subscription** — the base game became free in **Aug 2015** after 5M copies sold.

### (1) Base game free — no subscription
- Wikipedia: "the same **lack of subscription fees** that distinguished its predecessor… over 5 million copies sold, at which point the base game became free-to-play." Official header: "**Play for Free** | MMORPG." [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guild_Wars_2)

### (2) Expansions — release order & price
| # | Expansion | Year | Approx. price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Heart of Thorns** | 2015 | ~$39.99 |
| 2 | **Path of Fire** | 2017 | ~$39.99 |
| 3 | **End of Dragons** | 2022 | ~$39.99 |
| 4 | **Secrets of the Obscure** | 2023 | ~$29.99 |
| 5 | **Janthir Wilds** | 2024 | ~$39.99 |
| 6 | **Visions of Eternity** | 2025 | ~$39.99 |

*(Note: "Rise of the Dragon Knight" is not a GW2 expansion; "The Icebrood Saga" is a free Living World season.)* Prices are approximate — **confirm exact 2026 prices at checkout** (account.guildwars2.com/store). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guild_Wars_2)

### (3) Core gameplay
- **Dynamic Event System** — persistent world events that succeed/fail and ripple into each other (replaces static questing). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guild_Wars_2)
- **PvE / Personal Story** (level scaling to zone difficulty), **Fractals** (dungeons), **Raids** & **Strike Missions**, **World vs World** (persistent realm-vs-realm), and balanced **PvP**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guild_Wars_2)
- 8 crafting disciplines (2 at a time), **mastery tracks** that unlock progression.

### (4) Why it fits a small-time / casual player
- **No subscription, no time-gated gear** — play as much or as little as you want.
- **Drop-in/drop-out** — join events and WvW on the fly; no mandatory daily/weekly checklist.
- **No pay-to-win** — paid expansions grant content/access, not power; the Crown Store is largely cosmetic/convenience.

### (5) Gear / progression persistence (no resets)
- **No gear obsolescence** — once you earn/buy gear, it **stays viable** forever; you never re-grind it when new content drops. This is GW2's defining design principle.
- Account-wide **mastery points, crafting levels, achievements, unlocked gear, story completion** persist permanently. No seasonal resets. *(High-confidence from the confirmed free-to-play / one-time-purchase model.)*

### (6) 2026 news
- **June 2026: Guild Wars 3 announced.** ArenaNet is **pausing GW2 expansion development** to focus on the sequel, but "**Guild Wars 1 and 2 will continue to get updates**" and "We are not replacing your favorite games." [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/guild-wars-3/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guild_Wars_2)
- GW3 "won't have a battle-pass fee buried in it… We're not gonna hold their time hostage." [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/guild-wars-3/)

> **Must know:** GW2 is a **mature, complete, low-pressure** game to ease into — and buying the free base game grants **lifetime access** to all permanent content.

# 3. Final Fantasy XIV (FFXIV) — Best Story

**Links:** [EU Product/Pricing](https://eu.finalfantasyxiv.com/product/) · [Lodestone (news)](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIV)

### What it is
Square Enix MMORPG (director Naoki Yoshida) with a rich, story-driven main scenario. **Subscription-based**, but with a **generous free trial**.

### (1) Subscription model (verified pricing)

### (2) Free trial — generous

### (4) Why it fits a small-time / casual player
- **Story-rich** — the 2.0 → Dawntrail scenario is one complete epic ("one of the greatest games of all time"; 30M+ players). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIV)

- **No pay-to-win** — subscription funds free content patches every ~3 months; gear is earnable via gameplay; the paid store is cosmetic only.

### (5) Cross-server & housing
- **Cross-server characters** (up to 40 per data center), **Free Communities** (cross-server groups since patch 7.1, 2024), and **Free Company housing** (build a home with friends). [Official](https://eu.finalfantasyxiv.com/product/)

### (6) 2026 news
- **Nintendo Switch 2 version launched Aug 4, 2026** (50% discount for existing users). [Lodestone](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/)
- **Patch 7.55** (28 Jul 2026) "Occult Crescent"; **Evercold** expansion announced for 2027 at Fan Fest 2026. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIV)

> **Must know:** FFXIV **requires a subscription** after the free trial — the one trade-off vs ESO. But it's the **best story/content in the genre** and you can play entirely at your own pace with no P2W.

# 4. Elder Scrolls Online (ESO) — Your Known Fave

**Links:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_Online) · [UESP Buying Guide](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Buying_Guide) · [UESP Chapters](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Chapters)

### What it is
ZeniMax/BSG MMORPG, launched **4 Apr 2014** with a *mandatory* subscription, then rebranded **"Tamriel Unlimited" (17 Mar 2015)** to a **buy-to-play model with an OPTIONAL subscription**. Sold **15M+ units**, **$2B+ revenue** (2024). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_Online)

### (1) Buy-to-play, free-to-start, no required subscription
- Optional **"ESO+"** grants access to all current/future DLC, **1,650 Crowns/month**, and **10% faster progression**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_Online)

### (2) Chapters — real names & price
- **8 Chapters:** **Morrowind, Summerset, Elsweyr, Greymoor, Blackwood, High Isle, Necrom, Gold Road.** [UESP](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Buying_Guide)
- **List price:** **$39.99** (standard) / **$49.99** (deluxe upgrade) per chapter. The **Gold Road Collection (~$17–20 on sale)** includes the base game + all 8 chapters. [UESP](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Buying_Guide)
- **ESO+** grants access to all chapters **except the most recent one**.

### (3) Gameplay
- **7 classes** (Dragonknight, Sorcerer, Nightblade, Templar, Warden, Necromancer, Arcanist), **7 crafting skill lines**, **3 alliances**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_Online)
- **PvP:** Alliance War in Cyrodiil, Imperial City, Battlegrounds (4v4), Dueling — the modern **Fixed PvP system** arrived in **Update 41 (March 2022)**. [UESP](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:PvP)
- **Dungeons** (3–6 players) and **Trials** (large-group raider content) form the endgame.

### (4) Why it fits small-time players
- No required subscription, huge solo questing content, play at your own pace, and ESO+ is optional. The **seasonal "Content Pass"** model offers consistent free updates.

### (5) 2026 news
- **Update 49 (9 Mar 2026):** removed monetization of vampire/lycanthropy cures from the Crown Store. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_Online)
- **Seasonal content model** since 2025 (moved away from annual chapters). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_Online)

> **Must know:** This is the game you already like — it **already fits all your criteria** (persistent, no mandatory subscription, casual-friendly). Reaffirming it as a safe baseline.

# 5. Eve Online — Sci-Fi Depth

**Links:** [Official](https://www.eveonline.com/) · [EVE Academy (how it works)](https://www.eveonline.com/eve-academy) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eve_Online) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/8500/EVE_Online/)

### What it is
A fully persistent space MMO set across **7,000+ star systems** in a **single shared universe** (main server "Tranquility"). Player-driven economy, famously **steep learning curve**.

### (1) Free-to-play vs. paid
- **Free tier = "Alpha Clone"** — a **permanent free-to-play "never-ending trial."** Restricted to 5M Skill Points/character, Tech I frigates–battleships, slower training, no multiboxing.
- **Omega (paid):** ~**$20/month** (Steam) — unlimited Skill Points, all **350+ ships** (incl. T2/T3/Capitals), double training speed, lower manufacturing taxes. [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/8500/EVE_Online/)
- *(A cheaper "Beta" tier was only a 2023 forum proposal — not current.)*

### (2) Real-time skill training — your time = progress
- Skills train **over real time, continuously, even while offline** — your invested time always counts. [EVE Academy](https://www.eveonline.com/eve-academy)
- **Immortal Capsuleer:** when your ship is destroyed, your mind records and you respawn in a new clone. [EVE Academy](https://www.eveonline.com/eve-academy)

### (3) Fully persistent, player-driven economy
- **7,800 star systems**, player crafting/trading/manufacturing, corporations & alliances. The **Bloodbath of B-R5RB** (21-hour battle) is "one of the largest and most expensive battles in gaming history." Exhibited at the **Museum of Modern Art**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eve_Online)

### (4) Steep-curve warning
- Steam tag "**Difficult**." Use the excellent free guides: **EVE University** and **Quill18**. The Alpha/Omega grind and skill caps are the main barrier. [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/8500/EVE_Online/)

### (5) 2026 news
- **Fanfest 2026 (15 May 2026, Reykjavík):** the "EVE is dying" phrase appeared in the Capsuleer Edda poem during the keynote — sparking community debate. [eveonline.com/news](https://www.eveonline.com/news)
- **Cradle of War** expansion (9 Jun 2026): Military Campaigns, 8 new ships, and the **first non-PvP region** of New Eden. [eveonline.com/news](https://www.eveonline.com/news)

> **Must know:** Go into EVE for **depth and a living economy**, not ease. Your time genuinely = progress (offline skill training), but the learning curve is brutal — budget real onboarding time.

## Decision Cheat-Sheet

- Want **lowest time pressure + guaranteed progress**? → **OSRS**
- Want **fantasy MMO feel, no subscription**? → **Guild Wars 2**

- Want to **keep playing what you already like** → **Elder Scrolls Online**
- Want **sci-fi depth + player economy** (accept a steep curve)? → **Eve Online**
