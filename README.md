# MMO Research — Finding the Right Game

> **Goal:** An MMO where **time invested = permanent progress** — level-ups and gear persist, with **no wipes, no seasonal resets, no prestige/level resets**. PC only. Genre-agnostic.
>
> **Player profile:** Comes from *Lineage 2*, *World of Warcraft*, *New World*, *Elder Scrolls Online*. Loved **ESO's model** — low time-sacrifice, forgiving of a small budget (~8 hrs/month), and enjoyable **without paying** (no pay-to-win wall).

**Last updated:** 2026-08-31

---

## The 3 Filters That Matter

1. **Persistent progress** — your character, level, and gear stick forever. No server/seasonal wipes, no "prestige" resets that lower your level, no forced gear resets.
2. **Small-time-friendly** — you can progress and enjoy the game with only a few hours a month.
3. **F2P-viable / no pay-to-win** — the game is fun without spending money, and spending money doesn't buy a competitive edge.

A game passes only if it satisfies **all three**.

## Best Matches for Your Profile

| Game | Pay model | Small-time friendly? | Must-know before starting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Old School RuneScape (OSRS)** | F2P option + ~$11/mo membership | ⭐ **Best fit** | Skill levels advance over **real time**, not grind speed — so 8 hrs/month still counts. No wipes on the permanent world. Fair, chill, and your progress is truly permanent. |
| **Guild Wars 2** | Buy-to-play — base game **free, no subscription ever** | ✅ Very | Dynamic world events let you play in short bursts. Progress & gear persist permanently. No subscription, no pay-to-win. Buy expansions only if you want extra endgame. |
| **Final Fantasy XIV** | Subscription ~$16/mo (or ~$4 for a single month) | ✅ Very | **Needs a subscription** — the one trade-off vs ESO. But it has the best story/content in the genre and lets you play at your own pace. Buy-once content, play at your own speed. |
| **Elder Scrolls Online** | Buy-to-play — F2P-viable, no subscription | ✅ Yes (your known fave) | Reaffirming your pick: persistent, no mandatory subscription, casual-friendly. A safe "stick with what works" baseline. |
| **Eve Online** | F2P trial + paid subscriptions | ⚠️ F2P-viable, steep curve | Fully persistent with the biggest player-driven economy. Skills train in **real time even offline** — your time = progress. Ruthless: ships/gear can be lost in PvP. Choose for depth, not ease. |

---

## Top Recommendations (short version)

1. **Old School RuneScape** — the purest "I spend 8 hours and it counts" experience. F2P works, no wipes. Best fit for your exact profile.
2. **Guild Wars 2** — a traditional fantasy MMO feel (like WoW/L2) **without** the grind-job and **without** any subscription.
3. **Eve Online** — if you're open to sci-fi and want real depth; go in knowing the learning curve is brutal and loss is real.

## Decision Cheat-Sheet

- Want **lowest time pressure + guaranteed progress**? → **OSRS**
- Want **fantasy MMO feel, no subscription**? → **Guild Wars 2**
- Want **best story/content** and don't mind a subscription? → **Final Fantasy XIV**
- Want **sci-fi depth + player economy**? → **Eve Online**
- Just want to **keep playing what you already like**? → **ESO**
